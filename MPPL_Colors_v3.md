## Rules
1. Black is ALWAYS the base
2. ONE neon per graphic only
3. Neon = headline color
4. Always add subtle glow on neon elements
5. Never put neons on white background

- MATCHDAY: Black + Neon Green + White
- PLAYER: Black + Neon Orange + White
