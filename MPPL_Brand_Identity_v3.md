### Secondary Colors

| Swatch | Name | HEX | Usage |
|--------|------|-----|-------|
| 🟡 | **Neon Yellow** | `#FFE600` | Highlights, scores, tags. |
| 🩷 | **Neon Pink** | `#FF1493` | Finals edition, womens league, specials. |
| ⬜ | **Net White** | `#F5F5F5` | Body text, clean zones only. |

### Color Logic — Premier League Neon System

**THE GOLDEN RULE:**
Black is ALWAYS the base.
Neons sit ON black — never on white, never on grey.
Black makes neons glow. White kills them.

**ONE NEON PER GRAPHIC:**
Pick ONE neon per post —
Green OR Orange OR Yellow.
Never mix two neons in one graphic.

**NEON = HEADLINE:**
Whatever neon you pick — the headline is in that neon.
Everything else is white or black.

**GLOW EFFECT:**
Every neon element has a subtle outer glow —
same color as the neon, 15-20px blur, 40% opacity.
Makes it feel lit from within. Like a stadium LED board.

### Color Combinations

| Combo Name | Colors | Used For |
|------------|--------|----------|
| **MATCHDAY** | Black + Neon Green + White | Match announcements |
| **FIRE** | Black + Neon Orange + White | Player spotlights |
| **GRAND FINALS** | Black + Neon Yellow + Neon Pink | Finals edition |
| **EDITORIAL** | Black + White only | Clean info posts |

### Team Color System
*(Premier League inspired)*
Each team in MPPL gets ONE assigned neon:
- Team 1 → Neon Green `#39FF14`
- Team 2 → Neon Orange `#FF6000`
- Team 3 → Neon Yellow `#FFE600`
- Team 4 → Neon Pink `#FF1493`
All teams share League Black as their base.

## 03. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM
### Inspired by: Premier League custom font + STP neon bold energy

### Display / Headlines
**Font:** Bebas Neue OR Anton
- All caps, condensed, electric
- Used for: Match titles, player names, announcements
- Size: 72pt — 140pt
- Color: **Neon Green #39FF14 OR Neon Orange #FF6000**
- Always with subtle neon glow effect

### Sub-headlines
**Font:** Montserrat ExtraBold / Black
- All caps, tracked wide
- Used for: Team names, labels, tags, scores
- Size: 20pt — 48pt
- Color: White #F5F5F5 or Neon Yellow

### Body
**Font:** DM Sans Medium / Regular
- Clean, modern, legible
- Used for: Descriptions, schedules, captions
- Size: 13pt — 18pt
- Color: White #F5F5F5 at 75% opacity

### Hindi / Regional
**Font:** Hind Bold
- Used for: Hindi copy, regional taglines
- Size: 18pt — 36pt
- Color: **Neon Orange #FF6000** — always

### Numbers / Scores
**Font:** Bebas Neue
- ALL numbers in MPPL use Bebas Neue
- Match scores, player numbers, stats
- Massive scale — let numbers dominate
- Color: Neon Green for wins, Neon Orange for highlights

### Typography Rules
- NEVER use thin or light fonts for headlines
- NEVER put neon text on white background
- ALWAYS add glow to neon headline text
- Hindi copy ALWAYS in Hind — never in English fonts
- Minimum body text: 13pt

## 04. VISUAL STYLE SYSTEM

### Style 1 — THE PLAYER CUTOUT
*(SLK + STP Padel reference)*
- League Black #080808 background
- Player action photo — B&W or high contrast desaturated
- Behind player: diagonal neon green OR neon orange
  color slash/glow — like a stadium spotlight
- Large Bebas Neue headline in neon overlapping player
- Subtle neon glow on headline
- MPPL logo top left, white version
- Used for: Player spotlights, match announcements

### Style 2 — THE COLOR BLOCK
*(Expo Futebol + Premier League reference)*
- Bold geometric color blocks:
  Neon Green block + Neon Orange block
  + Black block + Black block
- The neon blocks glow against black sections
- Bold text sitting in color zones
- MPPL crest prominent, centered
- Used for: Brand posts, schedule announcements

### Style 3 — THE GRID
*(STP Padel reference)*
- League Black background
- Multiple info blocks in one post
- Neon green OR neon orange accent lines as dividers
- Each cell: one stat, one fact, one tip
- Small MPPL logo watermark repeating at low opacity
- Used for: Tips, stats, engagement posts, carousels

### Style 4 — THE EPIC WIDE
*(Premier League broadcast reference)*
- Full bleed court/action photography
- Heavy dark overlay — photo almost silhouette
- ONE massive Bebas Neue headline in neon
- Neon glow on the headline
- Maximum drama, minimum clutter
- Used for: Launch posts, finals, major moments

## 05. LOGO BRIEF

### Concept: The Hexagon Crest
Hexagon shape — modern, unique, pickleball connection.
Not a circle. Not a shield. A HEXAGON.
Nobody else uses it. Makes MPPL instantly distinctive.

### Elements
1. **MPPL** — Bebas Neue / Anton, bold condensed,
   center dominant inside the hexagon
2. **Stylized pickleball paddle** — geometric,
   clean lines, NOT realistic cartoon.
   Below the MPPL lettermark.
3. **MP identity** — subtle line outline of
   Madhya Pradesh state shape as background
   element OR star/diamond motif
4. **Curved text** along top inner edge:
   "MADHYA PRADESH PICKLEBALL LEAGUE"
5. **"EST. 2026"** — small, bottom of crest

### Neon Crest Colors
- Hexagon border: **Neon Green #39FF14**
  with green outer glow (20px blur)
- "MPPL" text: **Neon Orange #FF6000**
  with orange outer glow
- Paddle silhouette: **White #F5F5F5**
- Background inside hexagon: **Black #080808**
- Curved text: White #F5F5F5

## 10. BRAND DO & DON'T

### DO ✅
- Black background ALWAYS — no exceptions
- ONE neon per graphic — discipline is key
- Bebas Neue for EVERY major headline
- Neon glow on every neon element
- Player action cutouts with neon color slashes
- Bold geometric neon color blocking
- Hindi copy on every post
- Make it feel like a NATIONAL league
- Electric. Glowing. Impossible to scroll past.

### DON'T ❌
- White or grey backgrounds — ever
- Mixing two neons in one graphic
- Soft, warm, muted tones — this is NEON
- Thin fonts for headlines
- Too much text in one graphic
- Realistic cartoon illustrations
- Generic stock photography
- Treating it like a local tournament
- Gradients — flat neon on black only
