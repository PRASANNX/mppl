## DESIGN DIRECTION — FROM REFERENCES

### Reference 1 — Micki 7 Sports Website
- Full bleed dark backgrounds — black/dark grey
- Player/athlete cutout photos bleeding into typography
- MASSIVE numbers — scores, stats dominate the screen
- Red accent color (#CC0000 style) for highlights
- Side navigation labels (rotated 90 degrees)
- Dramatic stat displays — 418 GAMES, 142 GOALS
- Alternating dark and red full-bleed sections
- Typography: Large condensed sans, mixed weights

### Reference 2 — Move App (move.com style)
- Rounded pill/card UI components
- Floating notification-style cards
- Clean modern sans typography
- Community feel

### MPPL Adaptation
- Base: Deep Court #0D0B1F
- Accent 1: Neon Green #39FF14
- Accent 2: Neon Orange #FF6000
- Massive Bebas Neue/Defen Sport numbers
- Player silhouettes with neon glow
- Alternating: Dark → Neon Green → Dark → Neon Orange sections
- Side nav labels rotated (like Micki 7)
- Floating stat cards (like Move app)

## DEVELOPER NOTES — MICKI 7 STYLE ELEMENTS

### Typography Scale
Hero H1: 96-120px Bebas Neue
Section H2: 64-80px Bebas Neue
Prize Pool Number: 120-180px Bebas Neue
Stats: 48-64px Bebas Neue
Sub-headlines: 20-28px Montserrat ExtraBold
Body: 16-18px DM Sans

### Micki 7 Specific Treatments
1. Side labels (rotated 90°):
   On hero section sides — "MADHYA PRADESH" rotated,
   neon green, small, tracked wide.

2. Large background numbers:
   Section numbers (01, 02, 03...) as massive
   background text at 3-5% opacity.
   Bebas Neue, 300-400px, same color as BG.

3. Diagonal photo cuts:
   Athlete/player silhouette photos cut
   at a 15-degree diagonal angle.
   Photo bleeds off the right edge.

4. Stat callouts:
   Numbers appear with small labels below —
   exactly like "418 GAMES" in Micki 7.
   Large number (Bebas Neue) + small label (DM Sans).

5. Red/neon accent strips:
   Thin 4px horizontal lines in neon green
   used as separators and emphasis.

### Animations
- Hero number: Count up on load
- Stats bar: Count up on scroll entry
- Prize pool: Slow zoom + count up
- Map dots: Pulse animation (CSS keyframes)
- Category cards: Slide up on scroll
- Neon elements: Subtle glow pulse

### Floating Cards (Move app style)
On hero section — 3 floating cards:
Card 1: "🏆 ₹18L+ Prize Pool" — white pill, top right
Card 2: "📅 June 2026" — dark pill, bottom left  
Card 3: "👥 Ages 10–60" — neon pill, center right
These float with a subtle CSS animation.
